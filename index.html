The Journey

From Forest
to Jar

Six unhurried steps separate a wild honeycomb deep in the Villupuram forests from the jar on your table. No factories. No shortcuts. No rush.

  1. 01 — The Forest

    Where wild bees build

    Deep in the Kalrayan foothills, wild bees build combs in ancient trees — feeding on forest flowers untouched by pesticides.

  2. 02 — Tribal Harvest

    Climbed, not farmed

    Indigenous harvesters climb with rope and smoke, taking only mature comb and always leaving enough for the colony to thrive.

  3. 03 — Natural Filtering

    Gravity does the work

    Honey drips through muslin cloth by gravity alone. Never heated, never pressed — pollen and enzymes stay alive.

  4. 04 — Quality Testing

    Proven pure, every batch

    Every batch is lab tested for purity, moisture and adulteration before it earns the Pure N Good name.

  5. 05 — Glass Bottling

    Sealed in glass, never plastic

    Hand-filled into glass jars in small batches — no plastic, no preservatives, nothing added, nothing removed.

  6. 06 — Your Home

    The forest, on your table

    From a wild comb to your kitchen shelf in weeks, not months — carrying the whole forest with it.

Everything in the jar

Ingredients

100% raw wild forest honey. That's the whole list. No sugar, no syrup, no flavouring, no preservatives.

Nutrition

Per 100g (approx.): Energy 320 kcal · Carbohydrates 79g · Natural sugars 76g · Protein 0.4g · Fat 0g. Contains natural pollen, enzymes, and trace minerals.

Shelf Life

Best within 24 months of bottling. Raw honey may crystallise over time — that's a sign of purity, not spoilage. Warm the jar gently in water to re-liquefy.

Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. No refrigeration needed. Always use a dry spoon.

Harvest Region

Wild forests surrounding Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu — including the Kalrayan and Gingee hill ranges. Each jar notes its harvest season.

Delivery

Ships across India in 3–7 working days, packed in recycled paper cushioning. Free shipping on orders above ₹999.

Certification

FSSAI licensed. Every batch is independently lab tested for purity, moisture content and absence of adulterants — reports available on request.
