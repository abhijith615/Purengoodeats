Our Beginning

Grown slowly.
Made honestly.

Growing up in agricultural Ooty, nestled in the lush expanse of the Western Ghats, food was grown, made, and processed slowly and honestly.

Ghee was churned by hand, milk came from free-grazing cattle, oils were pressed fresh from seeds, and every single meal carried the story of where it came from.

As modern life moved us away from those rhythms, convenience replaced care, and food lost its clear connection to source and soil. PureNgood was born to restore that connection — bringing homes back to the wisdom, simplicity, and integrity preserved by our family and friends across the Western and Eastern Ghats of Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

Product Description

Harvested from the deep forests of the Western and Eastern Ghats, our Deep Forest Raw Blossom Honey is collected from wild and semi-wild hives where bees forage on native forest blossoms. It is raw, unfiltered, and minimally processed to preserve its natural aroma, texture, and character. Each batch reflects the season, the forest flowers in bloom, and the traditional care of local harvesters who work close to the land.

Taste Notes

This honey has a rich, earthy sweetness with floral undertones and a warm, forest-like aroma. Depending on the season, the flavour may feel light and fragrant or deeper and more robust, with a naturally thick, complex finish. You may also notice subtle crystalline texture over time, which is natural in raw honey.

Suggested Use
  • Drizzle over idli, dosa, toast, pancakes, or fruit.
  • Stir into warm water, herbal tea, or milk.
  • Use in salad dressings, glazes, and marinades.
  • Pair with yogurt, cheese, or roasted nuts.
  • Enjoy by the spoonful as a natural sweetener.
