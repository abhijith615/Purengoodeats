01

Deep Forest Raw Blossom Honey

Unfiltered, unheated, and seasonal jars straight from the Ghats.

Description

Harvested from the deep forests of the Western and Eastern Ghats, our Deep Forest Raw Blossom Honey is collected from wild and semi-wild hives where bees forage on native forest blossoms. It is raw, unfiltered, and minimally processed to preserve its natural aroma, texture, and character. Each batch reflects the season, the forest flowers in bloom, and the traditional care of local harvesters who work close to the land.

Taste Notes

This honey has a rich, earthy sweetness with floral undertones and a warm, forest-like aroma. Depending on the season, the flavour may feel light and fragrant or deeper and more robust, with a naturally thick, complex finish. You may also notice subtle crystalline texture over time, which is natural in raw honey.

Suggested Use

  • Drizzle over idli, dosa, toast, pancakes, or fruit.
  • Stir into warm water, herbal tea, or milk.
  • Use in salad dressings, glazes, and marinades.
  • Pair with yogurt, cheese, or roasted nuts.
  • Enjoy by the spoonful as a natural sweetener.

02

Wooden-Press Edible Oils

Cold-pressed in traditional wooden presses to retain true aroma and nutrients.

Description

Our wooden press edible oils are prepared in small batches using traditional methods that preserve the natural character of the seeds and the purity of the oil. The slow pressing process helps retain flavour, freshness, and authenticity while honouring village food traditions. Each oil is handled with care from source to bottle.

Taste Notes

Expect a fresh, full-bodied, seed-forward flavour with a clean natural aroma. The taste is more traditional and earthy than refined oil, with a pleasant depth that supports home-style cooking. Depending on the seed, the oil may taste mild, nutty, or distinctly aromatic.

Suggested Use

  • Use for everyday cooking and tempering.
  • Add to chutneys, curries, and vegetable dishes.
  • Drizzle over cooked rice or poriyal.
  • Use in traditional recipes where flavour matters.
  • Best suited for home kitchens that prefer authentic taste.

04

Desi Ghee

Handcrafted daily from the milk of free-grazing, grass-fed cows.

Description

Our desi ghee is made in the traditional way, using milk sourced from trusted village farms and slow preparation methods passed down through generations. It is crafted to bring richness, purity, and the familiar comfort of homemade food. Every batch is made with care and respect for traditional dairy practices.

Taste Notes

The flavour is rich, nutty, and deeply satisfying, with a warm aroma and a smooth finish. Good desi ghee should feel clean yet indulgent, carrying the natural sweetness of milk solids cooked gently to perfection. It adds depth without overpowering the dish.

Suggested Use

  • Use for tadka, frying, and everyday cooking.
  • Add to rice, chapati, dosa, or pongal.
  • Stir into sweets, laddoos, and festive recipes.
  • Spread on hot bread or rotis.
  • Use as a finishing touch for simple comfort food.
